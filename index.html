Privacy Policy

Personal Information

We respect the privacy of our users. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information outside your device.

Permission Requests

For the app to function properly, BWATERY requires some permissions:

  • INTERNET — Used only to show live weather temperature and hydration tips.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — Allows the app to remind you to drink water at the correct time.

Data Storage

All your hydration data (intake, goals, history) is stored only on your own device using local storage. Nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to share feedback, which is optional.

About BWATERY

BWATERY is a simple, effective water intake tracker that helps you stay hydrated by giving you a clear daily goal, a playful bubble that fills as you drink, and gentle reminders so you never forget to hydrate.

🛠️ Tech Stack

  • HTML5 + CSS3 — structure & styling
  • Vanilla JavaScript — all app logic
  • Open-Meteo API — live weather temperature
  • Web Notifications API — hydration reminders
  • Google Fonts — Poppins + Fredoka
  • localStorage — offline data persistence

🌟 Benefits

  • Better focus, energy, and skin health
  • Stops forgetting to drink water
  • Works fully offline — no account needed
  • No sign up required — everything saved on your device
  • Simple, distraction-free, and fun
